# JR-Central station geometry and 2026 route identity correction

- MLIT 国土数値情報 N02-2024 (CC BY 4.0):
  https://nlftp.mlit.go.jp/ksj/gml/datalist/KsjTmplt-N02-2024.html
- Extracted JSON: https://github.com/antfu/mlit-json/blob/main/data/stations.json
- JR Central's 2025 notice confirms 池の浦シーサイド retired in March 2020:
  https://jr-central.co.jp/news/release/_pdf/000044331.pdf

There are 13 JR-Central conventional canonical route IDs and 412 distinct
active station IDs. 0 old records had
missing station objects; a total of 377 records were not geocoded before
this backfill. Existing shared station coordinates are preserved.

Fix two wrong cross-operator homonyms:
Gotemba 足柄 -> JRC-Gotemba-010 (not Odakyu Ashigara).
MinoAkasaka 荒尾 -> JRC-MinoAkasaka-002 (not Kyushu Arao-Kumamoto).
Retire JRC-Sangu-010 from the current 2026 route, leaving 10 stations.
The Takayama JR Central/JR West station border is 猪谷, index 35;
the first JR West-side station 楡原 has index 36.

All added points are selected from the 2024 N02 record by original Japanese
station name, exact railway, and operator. No latitudes/longitudes were
fabricated. This updates topology only: zero simulated timetable numbers,
operating dates, stop clocks, vehicle identity or position evidence.

Previously populated shared coordinates needing independent review:
Atami, Hatta, Haruda and Tatsuno; original physical station coordinates
were not overwritten in this update.
